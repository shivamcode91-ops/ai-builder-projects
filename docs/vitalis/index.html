Every wearable gives you numbers.
Vitalis gives you one.

A native iOS app that reads Apple Health and your InBody scans and turns them into a single north-star metric: Biological Age. Recovery, Strain and Sleep in the Whoop tradition, plus the body composition no wrist wearable can measure.

100% on-device. No backend, no accounts, no cloud. Which is also why this page can exist: the scoring model has no server to call, so it was portable to the browser — every number below is computed from the readings in the panel, right here.
